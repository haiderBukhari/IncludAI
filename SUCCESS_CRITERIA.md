# StimuSonic — Success Criteria

Goal: back qualitative impressions with at least a few measurable data points, since "demonstrated impact vs. claimed impact" is a scored dimension.

## 1. User engagement & delight (qualitative + one measured signal)
- Qualitative: neurodivergent tester reports the tool made stimming feel playful/empowering, not clinical.
- Measured: **1–5 self-rating of comfort/enjoyment, taken immediately before and immediately after a session.** Report both numbers, not just an average impression.
- Bonus evidence: with consent, a short recorded clip of the tester's actual in-the-moment reaction — stronger demo material than any screen recording.

## 2. Ease of use
- A first-time user (non-technical, no instructions beyond on-screen prompts) can complete the full loop — permission → calibration → move → see/hear live output — without external help.
- Validated by watching at least one person who has never seen the app try it cold.

## 3. Creative quality / perceptible link
- The connection between input and output must be obvious, not abstract: e.g. three sharp taps should visibly/audibly produce three distinct events (three strokes, three notes), not a generic blob.
- Validated by asking the tester directly: "did it feel like your movement made that?" — record their literal answer.

## 4. Personalization demonstrated
- At least one saved custom mapping (e.g. "shaking = calm blue landscape") is created during testing and shown taking effect on a later session, live in the demo.

## 5. Technical functionality (stated targets, report actual numbers)
- **Live visual/sound latency: target <100ms motion-to-output.** Measure and report actual.
- **AI capture (image + caption) completion: target <10s.** Measure and report actual.
- Reliable sensor capture on a real iOS device (not just desktop Chrome) — explicitly tested, not assumed.
- Demo shows a clear before/after: raw motion trace → generated output, so the AI's role is visible, not a black box.

## 6. Ethics & process (credibility signal for clinical judges)
- Consent obtained before testing: what's collected (motion only, no camera/audio recording), how it's used, that there's no "right or wrong" way to stim, and that the tester can stop anytime.
- If a tester is a minor, parental/guardian consent obtained and stated explicitly in the submission.
- Submission and in-app copy consistently frame the tool as creative expression — never as calming, soothing, or managing behavior.

## Reporting format for submission
For each numbered section above, state: **target → actual result → tester quote or observation.** This turns "we think it worked" into a checklist judges can verify against, and directly supports the "impact demonstrated, not just claimed" scoring criterion.
